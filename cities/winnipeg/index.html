Winnipeg neighbourhoods

Explore Winnipeg’s neighbourhoods →

Winnipeg (Manitoba) is one of the 50 Canadian cities that Statistics Canada divides into census tracts, small neighbourhoods of about 2,500 to 8,000 people. It has 187 of them. The interactive map colours every tract by income, rent, age, where people were born, language and more, and compares Winnipeg with the other 49 cities.

  • 834,678people in 2021
  • $83,000median household income (2020), 27th of 50 cities
  • 34.3%of households rent their home
  • 19.3%of households spend 30% or more of income on shelter
  • 25.4%of residents are immigrants
  • 39.2median age

  • 25.1%have a mother tongue other than English or French

Income across Winnipeg’s neighbourhoods

Median household income ranges from $37,600 in the lowest tract to $159,000 in the highest, with half of tracts above $84,000. 2 tracts have no published income figure (Statistics Canada suppresses small or low-response areas).

Where people were born

25.4% of Winnipeg’s residents are immigrants, and 5.7% arrived between 2016 and 2021. The most common places of birth among them:
