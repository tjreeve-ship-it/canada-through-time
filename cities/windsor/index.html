Windsor neighbourhoods

Explore Windsor’s neighbourhoods →

Windsor (Ontario) is one of the 50 Canadian cities that Statistics Canada divides into census tracts, small neighbourhoods of about 2,500 to 8,000 people. It has 82 of them. The interactive map colours every tract by income, rent, age, where people were born, language and more, and compares Windsor with the other 49 cities.

  • 422,630people in 2021
  • $82,000median household income (2020), 29th of 50 cities
  • 27.5%of households rent their home
  • 17.5%of households spend 30% or more of income on shelter
  • 23.3%of residents are immigrants
  • 42.4median age
  • 23.6%belong to a visible minority group
  • 22.7%have a mother tongue other than English or French

Income across Windsor’s neighbourhoods

Median household income ranges from $34,000 in the lowest tract to $145,000 in the highest, with half of tracts above $86,000. 1 tract has no published income figure (Statistics Canada suppresses small or low-response areas).

Where people were born

23.3% of Windsor’s residents are immigrants, and 3.8% arrived between 2016 and 2021. The most common places of birth among them:
