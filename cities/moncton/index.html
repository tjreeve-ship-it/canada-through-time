Moncton (New Brunswick) is one of the 50 Canadian cities that Statistics Canada divides into census tracts, small neighbourhoods of about 2,500 to 8,000 people. It has 36 of them. Income across Moncton’s neighbourhoods

Median household income ranges from $42,800 in the lowest tract to $120,000 in the highest, with half of tracts above $73,000. 1 tract has no published income figure (Statistics Canada suppresses small or low-response areas).
