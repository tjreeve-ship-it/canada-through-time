Sault Ste. Marie neighbourhoods

Explore Sault Ste. Marie’s neighbourhoods →

Sault Ste. Marie (Ontario) is one of the 50 Canadian cities that Statistics Canada divides into census tracts, small neighbourhoods of about 2,500 to 8,000 people. It has 24 of them. The interactive map colours every tract by income, rent, age, where people were born, language and more, and compares Sault Ste. Marie with the other 49 cities.

  • 76,731people in 2021
  • $74,000median household income (2020), 44th of 50 cities
  • 30.3%of households rent their home
  • 15.8%of households spend 30% or more of income on shelter
  • 8.1%of residents are immigrants
  • 47.2median age
  • 4.3%belong to a visible minority group
  • 7.8%have a mother tongue other than English or French

Income across Sault Ste. Marie’s neighbourhoods

Median household income ranges from $34,800 in the lowest tract to $120,000 in the highest, with half of tracts above $70,500. 1 tract has no published income figure (Statistics Canada suppresses small or low-response areas).
