Chilliwack (British Columbia) is one of the 50 Canadian cities that Statistics Canada divides into census tracts, small neighbourhoods of about 2,500 to 8,000 people. It has 48 of them. The interactive map colours every tract by income, rent, age, where people were born, language and more, and compares Chilliwack with the other 49 cities.

  • 14.5%of residents are immigrants

Income across Chilliwack’s neighbourhoods

Median household income ranges from $42,000 in the lowest tract to $126,000 in the highest, with half of tracts above $82,000. 14 tracts have no published income figure (Statistics Canada suppresses small or low-response areas).
