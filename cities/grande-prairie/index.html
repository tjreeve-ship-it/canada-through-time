Grande Prairie (Alberta) is one of the 50 Canadian cities that Statistics Canada divides into census tracts, small neighbourhoods of about 2,500 to 8,000 people. It has 14 of them. The interactive map colours every tract by income, rent, age, where people were born, language and more, and compares Grande Prairie with the other 49 cities.

  • $102,000median household income (2020), 2nd of 50 cities
  • 34.3%of households rent their home
  • 22.4%of households spend 30% or more of income on shelter
  • 13.2%of residents are immigrants

Income across Grande Prairie’s neighbourhoods

Median household income ranges from $52,000 in the lowest tract to $125,000 in the highest, with half of tracts above $101,500. 2 tracts have no published income figure (Statistics Canada suppresses small or low-response areas).
