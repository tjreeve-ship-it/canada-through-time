Saskatoon (Saskatchewan) is one of the 50 Canadian cities that Statistics Canada divides into census tracts, small neighbourhoods of about 2,500 to 8,000 people. It has 65 of them. The interactive map colours every tract by income, rent, age, where people were born, language and more, and compares Saskatoon with the other 49 cities.

Income across Saskatoon’s neighbourhoods

Median household income ranges from $50,800 in the lowest tract to $146,000 in the highest, with half of tracts above $88,500. 1 tract has no published income figure (Statistics Canada suppresses small or low-response areas).

Where people were born

18.0% of Saskatoon’s residents are immigrants, and 5.6% arrived between 2016 and 2021. The most common places of birth among them:
