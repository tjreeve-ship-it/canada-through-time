Montréal neighbourhoods

Montréal (Quebec) is one of the 50 Canadian cities that Statistics Canada divides into census tracts, small neighbourhoods of about 2,500 to 8,000 people. It has 1,004 of them. The interactive map colours every tract by income, rent, age, where people were born, language and more, and compares Montréal with the other 49 cities.

  • 4,291,732people in 2021
  • $76,000median household income (2020), 40th of 50 cities
  • 45.6%of households rent their home
  • 19.6%of households spend 30% or more of income on shelter
  • 24.3%of residents are immigrants
  • 40.8median age
  • 27.2%belong to a visible minority group
  • 23.3%have a mother tongue other than English or French

Income across Montréal’s neighbourhoods

Median household income ranges from $23,400 in the lowest tract to $360,000 in the highest, with half of tracts above $74,000. 18 tracts have no published income figure (Statistics Canada suppresses small or low-response areas).

Where people were born

24.3% of Montréal’s residents are immigrants, and 3.9% arrived between 2016 and 2021. The most common places of birth among them:
