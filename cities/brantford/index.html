Brantford (Ontario) is one of the 50 Canadian cities that Statistics Canada divides into census tracts, small neighbourhoods of about 2,500 to 8,000 people. It has 32 of them. The interactive map colours every tract by income, rent, age, where people were born, language and more, and compares Brantford with the other 49 cities.

Income across Brantford’s neighbourhoods

Median household income ranges from $40,800 in the lowest tract to $159,000 in the highest, with half of tracts above $95,000. 1 tract has no published income figure (Statistics Canada suppresses small or low-response areas).
