Abbotsford–Mission (British Columbia) is one of the 50 Canadian cities that Statistics Canada divides into census tracts, small neighbourhoods of about 2,500 to 8,000 people. It has 43 of them. Income across Abbotsford–Mission’s neighbourhoods

Median household income ranges from $54,000 in the lowest tract to $141,000 in the highest, with half of tracts above $104,000. 3 tracts have no published income figure (Statistics Canada suppresses small or low-response areas).
