Sarnia (Ontario) is one of the 50 Canadian cities that Statistics Canada divides into census tracts, small neighbourhoods of about 2,500 to 8,000 people. It has 26 of them. The interactive map colours every tract by income, rent, age, where people were born, language and more, and compares Sarnia with the other 49 cities.

Income across Sarnia’s neighbourhoods

Median household income ranges from $47,200 in the lowest tract to $144,000 in the highest, with half of tracts above $84,000. 1 tract has no published income figure (Statistics Canada suppresses small or low-response areas).
